## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a **Master’s student in Data Science at EPFL**, passionate about **reasoning in large language models**, **discrete diffusion generative models**, and **planning theory in generative systems**.  

My research bridges **discrete diffusion** and **autoregressive models** for complex reasoning and planning, exploring how structured latent representations can enhance model collaboration and efficiency.  

I thrive at the intersection of **machine learning theory**, **generative modeling**, and **AI reliability**, aspiring to advance reasoning architectures that go *beyond autoregression*.
